Mesodisk is a local Windows disk utility.

It does not use telemetry, analytics, or network access.

Administrator rights are required because Windows only allows elevated processes to clear, partition, and format physical disks, and to read S.M.A.R.T. data.

Formatting is destructive. Mesodisk blocks disks that were present when it started, boot/system disks and virtual disks, and only formats while armed. It disarms itself after 15 minutes without a new disk.
